The celebrations

Every event is at the kalyana mandapam in Peddapuram. Each has its own colours, so the photographs come out beautifully.

At the muhurtham

For friends at their first Telugu wedding, here is what happens on the night of 4 December, in order.

  1. Jeelakarra Bellam

    జీలకర్ర బెల్లం

    At the exact muhurtham the cloth between them is lowered, and they place cumin and jaggery on each other's heads. This is the moment they are married.

  2. Mangalya Dharanam

    మాంగల్య ధారణ

    Prasad ties the mangalsutram around Yamini's neck with three knots, while the nadaswaram plays.

  3. Talambralu

    తలంబ్రాలు

    They shower each other with turmeric rice. It turns into a friendly contest, and everyone picks a side.

  4. Saptapadi

    సప్తపది

    Seven steps around the sacred fire, each with a promise.

  5. Arundhati Nakshatram

    అరుంధతి నక్షత్రం

    The couple are shown the Arundhati star, a symbol of a lifelong bond, in the night sky above the mandapam.
