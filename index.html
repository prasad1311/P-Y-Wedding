Moments to watch for

A short guide for friends joining their first Telugu wedding.

౧

Jeelakarra Bellam

జీలకర్ర బెల్లం

At the exact muhurtham, the couple place a paste of cumin and jaggery on each other's heads, for a life that holds both the bitter and the sweet.

౨

Mangalya Dharanam

మాంగల్య ధారణ

Prasad ties the mangalsutram around Yamini's neck with three knots while the nadaswaram plays.

౩

Talambralu

తలంబ్రాలు

The two shower each other with turmeric rice. It is usually the most playful moment of the night.

౪

Saptapadi

సప్తపది

Seven steps taken together around the sacred fire, each with a promise.

౫

Arundhati Nakshatram

అరుంధతి నక్షత్రం

The couple are shown the Arundhati star, a symbol of a lifelong bond. With a night muhurtham, the sky is right above us.
